Tide Tossers Privacy Policy

SwipeFinch LLC · Effective November 14, 2026

In short: Tide Tossers does not collect, store or share any personal information. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Tide Tossers is a game made by SwipeFinch LLC, Clearwater, Florida, USA ("we"). This policy covers the Tide Tossers apps for Android and iOS and the web version of the game.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: levels cleared, stars, scores, field-guide cards unlocked and gadgets earned.
  • Your settings: sound, vibration and units (metric or imperial).

These are stored only in the game's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site data in your browser for the web version, deletes them.

Permissions

On Android the app asks only for permission to vibrate the phone, for haptic feedback. You can turn vibration off in the game.

Internet use

The apps contain the whole game and work offline; they send nothing about you over the internet. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load it, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address in its server logs, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use that information.

Purchases

If you buy the game or an in-game item, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Tide Tossers is made for everyone, including children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If we ever begin collecting any information, we will say so here before that version of the game is released.

## Art-accuracy notes

- **Atlantic blue tang** is drawn as an adult: all blue with fine darker lines and a pale tail spine. The spec mentioned a "yellow tail", but the yellow-tailed blue fish is the Pacific (palette) blue tang, a different species. Drawing that one would also invite comparisons with a well-known animated character. Adult Atlantic blue tangs have no yellow tail; their juveniles are entirely yellow.
- **Florida stone-crab trap** is drawn as a slatted box with a weighted concrete base, the common style in Florida.
- **Lionfish** are invasive in Florida, so they appear in the Keys levels and never in the Galápagos.
